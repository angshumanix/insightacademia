How it works

Explore. Reflect. Get in touch.

The website is a demonstration library. Full-pack availability and any purchase arrangements are discussed privately through Telegram.

01

Explore a demonstration

Browse an educational sample, read a worksheet and download the sample PDF.

02

Get in touch on Telegram

Ask about full-pack availability, contents, formats, compatibility, pricing, updates and licence terms.

03

Discuss the details privately

If an available full pack suits your needs, we agree the purchase terms through Telegram. Any payment link and file delivery are handled privately.

Clarify the details before payment

Ask about contents, formats, device compatibility, updates, licence terms and any other questions. All material information and applicable conditions should be provided before payment.

Pay what you think is fair

For available full packs, discuss an amount that feels fair and comfortable for your budget through Telegram. We agree the final purchase amount privately before sending a PayPal payment link.

International customers

Commercial full packs are offered only to eligible customers located outside India. We do not accept domestic Indian orders or offer domestic payment methods.

Private payment and delivery

If you decide to purchase an available full pack, we manually send a payment link from our existing PayPal Business account through Telegram for an eligible international payment. After verifying payment, we manually deliver the files through Telegram. The website does not process payment or deliver full commercial packs.
