What to send on Telegram

Send the study pack’s name or product code to @InsightAcademiaPacks. We will confirm the pack and agree a purchase amount that feels fair and affordable to you, then send a PayPal payment link in the conversation.

Pay what you think is fair

We believe useful learning resources should remain accessible. Each pack has a suggested USD range to help you decide. The range is guidance, not a compulsory minimum or maximum. Get in touch on Telegram to discuss an amount that feels fair and comfortable for your budget. We agree the final purchase amount before sending your PayPal payment link.

Payment and delivery

Complete payment on PayPal. Once we verify it, we manually send the downloadable files through Telegram. Delivery timing will be confirmed in the conversation before payment.
