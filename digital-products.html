The details

The details behind your download.

Delivery and digital-product policy framework for InsightAcademia downloadable study packs.

Policy draft for development review. Operational details and seller information require approval before this website is launched.

Product information

Review the pack contents, preview, file formats and suggested USD range before ordering. The amount is discussed and agreed through Telegram before a PayPal payment link is sent. A product code identifies the selected pack. Any compatibility requirements will be included in the product specifications.

Manual digital delivery

We confirm the expected delivery timeframe through Telegram before payment. Once payment has been verified, we manually send the files through the same official account.

File access and incorrect files

If a file is inaccessible, incomplete or different from the confirmed product, contact @InsightAcademiaPacks with the product code and a description of the issue so it can be reviewed.

Refunds, cancellation and duplicate payments

The final policy will explain how refund requests, cancellation, duplicate payments and delivery problems are handled. These terms require approval before sales begin and will reflect the rights applicable to the transaction.

Versions and updates

Each product will identify the version being supplied. Any included update entitlement will be stated on its page. Update commitments and replacement-file availability are awaiting approval.
