The details

Digital product policy

Purchase clarifications, delivery, finality and six-month updates.

Policy draft for review. Seller details and legal wording must be reviewed for the relevant seller and customer jurisdictions before launch.

Clarify before paying

Ask on Telegram about contents, formats, device compatibility, updates, licence terms and any uncertainty. Material product information and applicable conditions must be provided before payment. Any purchase amount is agreed privately under our “Pay what you think is fair” approach.

International purchase scope

Any full-pack purchase is limited to eligible customers located outside India. We do not accept domestic Indian orders or provide domestic payment methods. Payment links are issued privately from our existing PayPal Business account for eligible international payments.

Private payment and delivery

For an agreed available full pack we manually send a payment link from our existing PayPal Business account through Telegram for an eligible international payment. After confirming payment, we manually deliver the files through Telegram. Expected delivery timing is confirmed before payment.

Final purchases and refunds

Purchases of delivered digital study packs are final. We do not provide discretionary refunds for changes of mind after delivery. Applicable statutory consumer rights, remedies and payment-provider protections remain unaffected. This policy does not prevent an eligible dispute or chargeback.

For missing, inaccessible, incorrect or materially misdescribed files, contact us on Telegram with the product code and a minimal transaction reference so the issue can be reviewed and any applicable remedy addressed.

Six months of complimentary updates

Every purchased full study pack includes six months of complimentary updates, starting on the confirmed payment date. Updates released for that specific purchased product during the eligibility period are available to the original purchaser without additional payment.

Newly developed, separately sold products and unrelated study packs are not automatically included. No particular number or frequency of updates is guaranteed. Contact us through Telegram to request eligible updates and verify the original purchase.

Original purchaser eligibility

Purchased materials are licensed for the original purchaser’s personal educational use. Unauthorized recipients do not receive complimentary updates or purchaser benefits. Refer to the terms and conditions for the licence.
