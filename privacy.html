Privacy policy

Privacy for the free educational library and Telegram communications.

Website information

This free static educational website uses no analytics trackers, advertising tags, forms, accounts, mailing lists, cookies or local storage in its own code. Fonts and assets are served with the site. Cloudflare, the hosting provider, may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the site.

Telegram communications

Telegram is our ordinary public channel for demonstration enquiries, feedback, complaints and accessibility issues. We receive the messages and profile information you choose to share. Telegram processes information independently under its own policies. Phone-number visibility can be controlled in its settings. Ordinary cloud chats are not end-to-end encrypted; Secret Chats are separate.

Minimal retention

We keep enquiry information only as needed to respond and address any outstanding issue or applicable legal obligation. You may contact us on Telegram to request access, correction or deletion of information held by us. Deleting a chat does not erase files or independent copies already saved by participants. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Contact

InsightAcademia is operated by Angshuman Banerjee from India. Contact @InsightAcademiaPacks with privacy questions.

See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Telegram’s privacy policy.
