The details

A simple catalogue. A clear view of your data.

Privacy information for the InsightAcademia website, Telegram conversations and PayPal payments.

Policy draft for development review. Operational details and seller information require approval before this website is launched.

Using the website

This static website presents study-pack information. Its own code uses no analytics trackers, advertising tags, cookies or local storage. Fonts and visual assets are served with the site. The hosting provider may process technical request data, such as IP addresses and server logs, to deliver and secure the website.

Contact through Telegram

Customer communication takes place through @InsightAcademiaPacks. Messages and any profile details you share are visible to the account receiving them. Telegram processes conversations under its own policies. You can manage phone-number visibility and other privacy settings in Telegram.

Ordinary cloud chats are not end-to-end encrypted. Telegram’s separate Secret Chats feature provides end-to-end encryption. You can delete one-to-one messages for both parties, but downloaded copies and payment records are separate.

Paying through PayPal

Payment links are sent manually in Telegram. Payment details are entered on PayPal. PayPal processes payment data under its own policies and provides transaction information to the seller.

Order records and retention

The final policy will identify the seller, specify the minimum order information retained, explain the retention period and describe how privacy requests are handled. These operational details are awaiting approval before launch.

Questions about privacy

Contact InsightAcademia through the official Telegram account. See also Telegram’s privacy policy and PayPal’s privacy policy.
