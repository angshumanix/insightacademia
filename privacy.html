Privacy policy

Website information, private conversations and purchase records.

Policy draft for review. Seller details and legal wording must be reviewed for the relevant seller and customer jurisdictions before launch.

Website data

This static demonstration library uses no analytics trackers, advertising scripts, enquiry forms, customer accounts, mailing lists, cookies or browser storage in its own code. Fonts and visual assets are local. The hosting provider may process technical request data, including IP addresses and logs, to deliver and secure the site.

Telegram and PayPal

Telegram is our exclusive ordinary channel for customer enquiries, complaints and privately arranged digital delivery. Messages and profile information you share are visible to the receiving account. Telegram processes information under its own policies. Ordinary cloud chats are not end-to-end encrypted; Secret Chats are separate. Phone-number visibility is controlled through Telegram settings.

Any eligible international purchase is handled privately through our existing PayPal Business account. Domestic Indian orders are not accepted. Payment details are entered on PayPal, which processes them independently and supplies transaction information to the seller. Chat deletion does not erase downloaded files, independent copies or transaction records.

Minimal purchase records

For private purchases we retain the product identifier and version, confirmed payment date and transaction reference, agreed amount/currency, a minimal contact reference, delivery confirmation, the applicable terms version and the six-month update eligibility dates. Records may also include necessary update requests or dispute information.

These records are used to verify purchases, supply eligible updates, address payment disputes and meet legal recordkeeping obligations. They are held privately with restricted access, not published in the website repository. We do not request card details or identity documents for routine verification.

Retention and privacy requests

Website enquiries and privacy requests are handled through Telegram; enquiry information is kept only as needed to respond and address any outstanding issue. Purchase-record retention periods and accounting requirements will be finalized separately before the first paid sale. Records required for a legal obligation or an active dispute may need to be retained. Contact the official Telegram account with privacy questions.

See Telegram’s privacy policy and PayPal’s privacy policy.
