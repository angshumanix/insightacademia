# Initial free educational release

Owner-authorized scope: three free educational PDF demonstrations, brand/about, educational-use terms, privacy and Telegram demonstration questions/feedback. Public pages promote no orders, prices, payments, commercial availability or delivery. Earlier commercial policy source is preserved in FUTURE_COMMERCIAL_POLICY_SOURCE.txt and is not part of the deployment export.

Disclosure assessment for this version: the current Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 scope covers goods/services bought or sold over electronic networks and platforms for electronic commerce. This release offers no commercial product or purchase arrangement, and Telegram is presented for educational questions and feedback only. Its actual activities provide a defensible basis to classify this launch as free educational publishing, rather than relying on the absence of checkout as an automatic exemption. This is a scope-specific assessment, not a blanket legal exemption for the brand or future business. Reassess before commercial promotion or sales. The 2026 amendment commences 1 January 2027 and is not treated as already effective.

Operator name and India location are disclosed in the privacy page using known information; no PAN or invented address/contact detail is published. The policy explains host request processing, Telegram privacy, minimal enquiry retention and contact for privacy requests. No tracker, form, account or payment data is collected by the site.

The owner’s final launch instruction approves the free sample content/branding and deployment subject to verification. Approval flags apply to this free release only. Future commercial product approval, customer disclosures, tax/accounting and transaction records remain separate pre-sale requirements.

Repository sources, commercial drafts, data files and customer information are not uploaded. Origin HTML paths are retained, with an explicit root index alias and canonical URLs on insightacademia.org. Security headers are applied at the asset layer.
