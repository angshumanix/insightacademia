# Demonstration records and private full packs

Public input is data/demonstrations.json. Each entry has id, safe unique slug, status="demo", title, learning category, summary, teaching description, audience, contents, cover style, local preview with alt text, samplePdf, samplePages, updated date and approved flag. Its page and card state: “Demonstration material — not a complete commercial study pack.” No public pricing fields are allowed.

Only intentionally public educational samples belong in assets/. Current PDFs contain a teaching example and a worksheet, not full products. The sample PDF is downloadable from each detail page and embedded in the offline review. No full commercial file or link belongs in public metadata.

A future internal full-pack record can use the preserved suggestedLower/suggestedUpper USD pricing model and seven internal bands. Do not store actual confidential data in this public repository. Public generation ignores data/products.json, even if its records are marked ready. Readiness helpers and their tests remain as internal groundwork; they cannot turn a full product into a public listing.

Before publishing a new sample, review its educational accuracy, rights, accessibility and page count; set its own approval only after review. Approval of all demonstrations, seller details, policies and deployment arrangements is required for release. Default development output remains noindex.
