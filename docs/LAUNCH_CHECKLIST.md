# Two separate checkpoints

## Before publishing the demonstration-only website

- [ ] Approve the final sample content, original branding and public wording. Browser automation has passed; final owner visual review remains.
- [ ] Resolve the hosting arrangement and explicitly approve deployment. The GitHub Pages commercial-use question remains unresolved; an external payment flow does not resolve it.
- [ ] Complete a focused review of any seller-identification/contact/complaint disclosures applicable to this India-operated public catalogue and intended markets. Operator identity is already known; do not ask for it again or assume it is legally unnecessary. Keep personal identity out of prominent marketing; never publish PAN.
- [ ] Approve the public terms/privacy/sample-use policies and Telegram handling of website enquiries and complaints. Confirm who handles these communications and the minimal enquiry retention practice. Full-product transaction policy remains prospective.
- [ ] Set the existing seller-details, policies, library/per-sample and deployment approval flags only after those decisions. Keep noindex until an approved release build. No new integration is required.

There is no known failing functional website test. Assistive-technology checks and 200% zoom remain review items, not newly invented infrastructure requirements. This checklist does not authorize merging or publishing.

## Before the first privately arranged paid sale

- [ ] Create and approve the actual full product, contents, rights, formats/compatibility, licence, suggested private pricing and delivery expectation.
- [ ] Confirm the customer is located outside India and the destination/payment is eligible through the existing PayPal Business account. Decline domestic Indian orders; do not implement a domestic payment method.
- [ ] Complete the separate GST/export/accounting/invoicing review for actual transaction facts. Do not assume that every international payment is automatically zero-rated; confirm any registration, LUT, remittance evidence or destination obligations that apply.
- [ ] Provide required seller particulars, material product information, final amount, licence/refund/update terms and any legally required notices before payment.
- [ ] Finalize the minimal private purchase ledger, six-calendar-month eligibility dates, dispute evidence and applicable retention timetable. Keep records outside the public repository.

The PayPal Business account already operates. Do not turn these tasks into account-setup work, a CRM, helpdesk, compliance application or web payment integration. Free public samples and website development do not depend on finished commercial inventory.
