# Minimal private purchase-record procedure — draft

Keep the ledger outside this public repository and all static export folders, in encrypted storage accessible only to the seller's authorized operator. Never commit customers, conversations, receipts, transaction identifiers, backups or actual full packs. No real record or example containing personal data is included here.

Before payment, provide the available product's identifier/version, actual contents, formats, compatibility, personal-use licence, agreed amount/currency, expected delivery timing, finality/refund terms and six-month updates. Record the terms version confirmed in the conversation. Clarify uncertainties first.

After verifying payment in PayPal, record only:

- Product ID and version, confirmed payment timestamp/date and currency/amount.
- PayPal transaction reference (not card or bank details).
- Minimal Telegram chat/contact reference sufficient to respond and verify the original purchaser; a username alone may change.
- Terms/policy version, delivery date, supplied file version and a minimal delivery confirmation.
- Update eligibility start and end dates, and necessary update request/version history.
- Minimal unresolved-issue/dispute references where needed.

Calculate six calendar months from the confirmed payment date; if the target month lacks that day, use its last day. Confirm the chosen timezone and any legally required calculation convention before the first paid sale. Updates released for that specific product during eligibility may be requested by the original purchaser at no extra payment. New/unrelated separately sold products are excluded. No release frequency is promised.

For update requests, match the contact/product and transaction reference to the private ledger and verified PayPal record. Do not ask for card data, identification documents or entire financial statements for routine verification. Resolve mismatches privately and disclose no other purchaser's details. Unauthorized recipients receive no purchaser benefits.

Retain only the evidence reasonably needed for delivery, eligible updates, disputes and applicable legal obligations. Seller/customer jurisdictions and applicable tax/accounting/payment rules must determine exact retention periods before the first paid sale. Record a retention rule and review date; delete or minimize data when no valid need remains, while preserving applicable legal holds. Chat deletion does not erase mandatory transaction records. Restrict access and maintain an encrypted recovery copy under the same retention policy.

No automated customer database, public form, mail list or account system is required. Seller identity is now confirmed. Private purchase-storage responsibility and its retention timetable are pre-sale decisions. Public-site disclosure and enquiry-privacy review remain website-launch decisions; see LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md.
