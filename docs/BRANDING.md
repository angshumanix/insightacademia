# InsightAcademia identity

Original minimalist open-book outline with a drawn IA monogram. Deep pine #123E32, warm ivory #F7F5EF and muted gold #C0A05A remain the principal palette. Gold decoration is paired with darker #755B24 text on light backgrounds for readability.

Assets: assets/logo-dark.svg (dark artwork for light surfaces), assets/logo-light.svg (light artwork for dark surfaces), assets/emblem.svg (standalone pine emblem) and assets/favicon.svg (ivory emblem on pine). All are editable SVG with no external resources. The wordmark uses Georgia with a serif fallback; the monogram itself is drawn geometry. Header and footer use the corresponding logo variant.

Keep clear space around the book and wordmark. Preserve aspect ratio. The favicon is intentionally simpler than a complete wordmark. No stock art or institutional affiliation is implied. Final owner review and rendered browser checks remain required.
