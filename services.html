What we offer

Services built for real academic pressure

Custom writing support across every format and subject. All work is original, confidential, and tailored to your exact brief.

Thesis & Dissertation Support

Your most complex deliverable, expertly managed

End-to-end research and writing support for Masters and undergraduate dissertations. We work chapter-by-chapter, structured to your university’s specific guidelines, word count, and citation style.

  • Research question development and scoping
  • Literature review research and writing
  • Methodology design and chapter writing
  • Data analysis and results chapter
  • Discussion, conclusion, and abstract
  • APA, Harvard, Vancouver, or institutional formatting
Essay & Assignment Writing

Custom to your brief, your module, your grade

From 500-word reflective pieces to 5,000-word research essays. Every assignment is written from scratch around your brief and your institution’s marking rubric — argument-driven, properly cited, and in your academic voice.

  • Topic analysis and argument planning
  • Full-draft writing or structural rewriting
  • In-text citation and reference list
  • All subject areas and academic levels
  • 100% original, custom-written work
  • Rush options available for tight deadlines
Research Assistance

Find the evidence. Build the argument.

When you know what you want to say but need the academic substance behind it. We conduct systematic literature searches, identify research gaps, and synthesise evidence into structured, coherent academic support material.

  • Systematic literature review and search
  • Research gap identification and positioning
  • Secondary data sourcing and analysis
  • Annotated bibliography creation
  • Research framework and conceptual mapping
  • Source summary and synthesis documents
Proofreading & Editing

Your content, at its absolute best

You’ve written the work. We make it submission-ready. Grammar, academic tone, sentence clarity, paragraph flow, and formatting — professionally sharpened without altering your core argument or ideas.

  • Grammar, syntax, and punctuation
  • Academic register and tone
  • Sentence clarity and paragraph flow
  • Structural and organisational editing
  • Reference list formatting and checking
  • Word count reduction (if required)
Content & Business Writing

Professional writing beyond the classroom

Insight Academia also supports businesses and professionals with high-quality written content — blog posts, website copy, business reports, and professional documents, written to a standard that represents you well.

  • Blog posts and thought leadership articles
  • Website and landing page copy
  • Business reports and executive summaries
  • Professional proposals and pitches
  • LinkedIn and professional correspondence
  • SEO-aware content (on request)
