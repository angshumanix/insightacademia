The catalogue

Six tiers of structured study packs

What's inside

Frameworks, not vague advice

Every pack is built around a repeatable system. Here is a sample of what is actually inside.

Tier 1 · Micro

The 4-Step Quiz Strategy System

A sequence for any quiz format. Read the stem fully, predict your answer before looking at the options, eliminate systematically, then confirm by re-reading the stem with your choice inserted.

  • MCQ distractor logic and elimination rules
  • Fill-in-the-blank grammatical framing techniques
  • True or false qualifier analysis
  • Subject-specific worked examples
Tier 1 · Micro

The 4-Point Response Framework

A structure for any discussion post or short reflection. Hook, position, support, implication. Each part has a defined word count so the response stays inside the limit without losing substance.

  • A fully annotated worked example
  • Four subject-specific variations
  • A phrase toolkit for transitions and hedging
  • A fill-in-the-blank write-along template
Tier 1 · Micro

The FRAME System for In-Class Tasks

A 90-second checklist for any in-class activity: format, role, ask, minutes, evidence. Includes the STAR method for scenario questions and four named techniques for group discussion contributions.

  • Five activity type guides, including case studies and reflections
  • The participation grading decoder
  • Subject-specific worked examples
Tier 1 · Micro

The 5-Part Compressed Essay Framework

Built for the 400 to 500 word limit. Funnel opening, thesis, two developed argument points, a counter and rebuttal, and an echo close that does not repeat the thesis verbatim.

  • The thesis construction system and formula
  • Eight word-saving compression techniques
  • A fully annotated 460-word worked example
  • Four subject-specific examples

These four examples are drawn from Tier 1 packs. Higher tiers cover presentations, case analyses, research proposals, dissertation chapters, and full-length models.
