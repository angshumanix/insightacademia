Common questions

A few useful details.

The collection, the files and the process, explained in plain language.

What is a study pack?

A ready-made digital learning resource. A pack may include explanations, frameworks, worksheets and worked examples. The individual product page will describe its exact contents.

Are any study packs available yet?

The first collection is in preparation. Available products will be listed with approved contents, previews, file specifications and prices. Demonstration products in the design preview are illustrative.

How do I order a pack?

Choose an available pack and send its name or code to @InsightAcademiaPacks on Telegram. We send a PayPal payment link, verify the completed payment and manually deliver the files through Telegram.

What file formats will I receive?

The product page will list the included formats, such as PDF or editable document files. Check those specifications before ordering.

How soon will my files arrive?

Delivery is manual. We confirm the expected timeframe on Telegram before you pay, then send the files after verifying payment.

How can I use the materials?

Use them for independent learning and personal academic reference. Product-specific licence details will be shown before purchase. Follow the academic rules that apply to your course.

What if I have a problem with a file?

Contact @InsightAcademiaPacks on Telegram with the product code and a description of the issue. The digital-product policy explains the details to be finalized before launch.

How does privacy work?

The website uses a simple static catalogue. Conversations take place on Telegram and payments on PayPal. Read the privacy page for the distinctions between these platforms and their data handling.
